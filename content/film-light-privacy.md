## 我们不收集什么 / What We Don't Collect

本应用不含任何账号注册、登录或云同步功能，不含分析统计、广告或崩溃上报类第三方软件开发工具包（SDK），不会将您的照片、位置、设备信息或使用行为发送到我方或任何第三方服务器。

*The app has no account, login, or cloud-sync system. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. It never sends your photos, location, device information, or usage behavior to our servers or any third party's.*

---

## 相机 / Camera

用于拍摄照片和实时测光预览。拍摄产生的照片和参数记录保存在您设备本地的应用私有存储空间中，不会上传。

*Used for capturing photos and live metering preview. Photos and their shooting metadata are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device and are never uploaded.*

---

## 定位 / Location

若您授权定位权限，本应用会记录拍摄时的地理位置，并将其保存在设备本地的照片详情记录中，同时用于向 Apple 的 WeatherKit 服务查询拍摄地点的天气信息。该定位数据仅保存在您的设备上，不会发送至我方服务器；向 WeatherKit 的天气查询由 Apple 的系统框架直接处理，我方不会经手或留存该请求内容。

拒绝定位权限不影响测光与拍摄功能，仅代表照片详情中不会记录位置与天气信息。

*If you grant location permission, the app records the location at the time of capture and stores it locally in that photo's detail record, and also uses it to query weather at the shooting location via Apple's WeatherKit service. This location data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers; the WeatherKit lookup is handled directly by Apple's system framework, and we never see or retain that request.*

*Declining location permission does not affect metering or capture — it only means location and weather won't be logged for your photos.*

---

## 照片库 / Photo Library

本应用仅请求"添加"权限（write-only），用于将您编辑后的照片导出到系统相册。本应用不会读取您相册中已有的照片或其他内容。

*The app only requests add-only (write) access to your Photo Library, used to export your edited photos to it. The app never reads existing photos or other content from your library.*

---

## 数据存储与删除 / Data Storage & Deletion

所有照片、拍摄参数、胶卷日志信息均保存在您设备本地的应用沙盒内，不经过任何服务器中转或备份到我方系统。

删除照片时，照片先移入应用内部的回收站，30 天后自动永久清除；删除后可立即通过"撤销"恢复。卸载本应用会清除应用私有存储空间内的全部数据。

*All photos, shooting parameters, and film-roll log data are stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device — never relayed through or backed up to any server we operate.*

*Deleted photos first move to an in-app trash and are permanently erased after 30 days; an "Undo" prompt lets you restore a photo immediately after deletion. Uninstalling the app erases all data in its private storage.*

本应用不面向儿童设计，也不含任何专门收集儿童个人信息的功能。

*The app is not directed at children and contains no feature designed to collect personal information from children.*
