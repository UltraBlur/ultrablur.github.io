## Where it comes from

Finlayson & Süsstrunk (2000) fitted this spectral-sharpening matrix to Lam's visual corresponding-colour dataset to replace HPE. Its three effective sensitivity curves are a mathematical optimisation result rather than measured camera sensors or physiological cone fundamentals; the paper compares it with other CATs on corresponding-colour prediction.
