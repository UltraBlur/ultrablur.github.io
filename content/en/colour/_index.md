This is a colour space reference. Each entry records a space's gamut (primaries and white point), its RGB→XYZ matrix and its transfer function, and reproduces the encoding and decoding functions verbatim so that every figure can be recomputed.

Entries fall into two classes: **Scene Referred** (cameras, film, ACES and working spaces) and **Display Referred**.

Data are taken from manufacturer specifications and standards documents. Entries backed by a primary document carry source links; where no such document could be found, none are listed.
